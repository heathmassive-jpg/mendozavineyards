Garden Home, Guesthouse on 27-Acre Farm

Lovely Farm Close to Town

This 27-acre farm (11 hectares) is located about 15 minutes (12 km) from San Rafael. It features a nice 2-bedroom, 1 bath home with a 2-bedroom guest house in a lovely garden setting.

It's a quiet country location off a main highway with access on a pretty garden lane that affords privacy. Beautiful trees, flowers and bushes surround the home.

There are are at least 20 acres that could be put into almost any type of agriculture common in the area: Orchard, vineyard, olives, alfalfa walnuts or annual vegetables.

The farm is close to Valle Grande where there are a number of activities such as river rafting, horseback riding, boating etc. The property is also close to a number of wineries.

This land also has potential for tourism income such as rental cabanas or other development.

Property has all utilities including electric, natural gas, high speed internet, public water.
