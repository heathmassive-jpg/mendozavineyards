Cabernet Sauvignon Vineyard with Pool Home on 48-Acre Farm

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This 48-acre farm has 7 hectares (17 acres) of grapes, with 5.5 hectares (13.5 acres) in Cabernet Sauvignon and 1.5 hectares (3.5 acres) in muscatel and ceresa common grapes.

The grapes have produced an average over the past ten years of 61,000 kilos per year. However this year there was some frost damage, but they were able to limit the damage with frost control to about 50 percent.

There are 100 quince trees that are absolutely loaded this year and produced 10,500 kilos of fruit last year.

There are also about 15 large mature olive trees that gave 295 kilos of olives this year which the owners traded to the Yacanelo olive oil factory for 38 liters of oil (the equivalent of 80 bottles of oil at 16 ounces each).

Ten hectares (25 acres) of land remain uncultivated.

The house has a well-kept interior and has 2 bedrooms and 1 bath with about 1,000-square-feet of space. It is served by city water and has good electric. There is a swimming pool next to the home, and on the side next to the pool is a grape arbor that produces an ample yearly harvest of table grapes.

A relatively new garage/barn has just over 1,000 square feet (32x32 feet) and can also be used as a workshop area.

The farm has water irrigation rights from irrigation canals in front of the farm and also have a deep irrigation well with a depth of 62 meters and a submersible pump that is located at a depth of 25 meters.
