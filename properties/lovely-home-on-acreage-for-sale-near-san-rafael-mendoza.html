La Llave Vieja, San Rafael, Mendoza

Lovely Home on Good Land with Olive Trees

Fenced and Gated Rural Land

This 12.5 acres farm (5 hectares) is located about 25 minutes from the center of San Rafael on a paved road near a public school.

This 2-bedroom and 2-1/2 bath home was very well built by a former contractor using a civil engineer who sourced premium materials. A stove and refrigerator are included and it has mini-splits for A/C and heating in addition to a fireplace.

There are about 50 mature olive trees, and the owner leases out the land for oats to a neighbor to cover the taxes and irrigation fees so the property has very little maintenance costs. The contract is on an annual basis, so a new owner could plant what they like, such as vineyard, olives, fruit trees or annual vegetables.

The owner is selling because of some recent health issues and has another home in town where he would relocate.

There is a garage with wood shop which measures 610 square feet (60 m2) with a little shop and there is an open shed for firewood and other items. Both were constructed in the style of the home. It's located in La Llave Vieja, east of San Rafael.
