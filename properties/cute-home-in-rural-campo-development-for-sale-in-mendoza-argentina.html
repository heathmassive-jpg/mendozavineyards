Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael, Mendoza

Cute 1-Bed Pool Home in Campo Estate

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This lovely 1-bedroom home is located in a rural rustic-campo development close to nature in Pueblo Benegas outside San Rafael, Mendoza. It is down the road from the multi-million-dollar Algodon Golf and Wine Estates, and on the way to the Las Lenas ski resort.

Called "Casa Peumayen," the house is 635 square feet plus large patio & pool, so it seems larger. Within the Pueblo Benegas country club there is a goat and sheep cheese project and a gourmet restaurant with signature cuisine. There is a large lagoon nearby.

The kitchen has a view of the Sierra Pintadas (Painted Hills) and the pool is just off the patio.

"The club invites you to walk its different trails in the middle of nature full of chanar and aguaribay forests, where you will find views of the mountains and our beautiful lagoon.

"The House is intertwined with jarillas, chanares, cactus and olive trees, the house rises facing the mountain, built of stone, in total connection with the environment but without losing the homely warmth.

"The area is surrounded by tourist attractions for adventures, sports and is close to many prestigious wineries and restaurants where you can to enjoy their wines and exquisite dishes."
