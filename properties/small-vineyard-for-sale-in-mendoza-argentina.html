Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael, Argentina

Small Malbec Vineyard on 12 Acres in Tourist Area with Room to Build

Profitable Vineyard!

This hobby vineyard is located on 12.3 acres in Cuadro Benegas, a popular area for tourists in San Rafael, Mendoza.

An excellent caretaker lives next door to the property and would like to remain on with a new owner.

The grapes have been sold annually to some of the top wineries in the area, including the Bianchi winery who exports worldwide.

The annual vield ranges up to 15,000 kilos per year, which is equal to almost 15,000 bottles of wine. You would also have the option of having a local winery elaborate wine for you and then you private label, or construct a boutique wiinery on the site.

In 15 years the vineyard has never lost money. The owner lives in Europe and has maintained things from afar without problems.

The owner says in a good year after expenses there is a profit of about US$3,400.

The harvest in March of 2025 was 10,540 kilos of Malbec grapes.

The farm is not far from the multi-million- dollar Algodon wine and golf estate.

There is access to the property from good roads on two sides. Electric, water and services are available at the roads. Water irrigation is good and sufficient.
