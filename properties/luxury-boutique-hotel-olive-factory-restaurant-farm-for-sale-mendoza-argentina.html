Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael, Mendoza

Luxury Boutique Hotel, Olive Oil Company, Olive Groves & Restaurant

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This wonderfully diverse income property includes a luxury boutique hotel, olive groves, a craft olive oil production facility and small restaurant with indoor and outdoor seating which is currently leased. It is located on 32 acres in a popular tourist area on the ski route to Las Lenas outside San Rafael, Mendoza, nestled across the road from the multi- million-dollar Algodon Golf and Wine Estates.

The hotel has a 9.8-star visitor rating and covers more than 6,000 square feet with six finely-appointed rooms.

Above the hotel living quarters is a large roof- top patio overlooking the olive groves and affording a nice view of the Painted Hills (Sierra Pintadas). The view from the pool also has a good view of the groves and the Sierra Pintadas.
