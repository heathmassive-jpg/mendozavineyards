Las Paredes,San Rafael, Mendoza

Well-Kept Fine Wine Vineyard with Pool and Cabana in Great Location

Tractor & Implements Included

This well-kept vineyard producing fine wine grapes has a great location at the entrance of San Rafael on the route across the desert from the city of Mendoza.

There are 13.8 acres (5.6 hectares) of vineyard under netting with 5 hectares in Malbec and 8.8 acre in Merlot. Previously the Valentine Bianchi Winery (the best-known winery in San Rafael) purchased grapes from this vineyard for their high-end Merlot "Particular" which received excellent reviews.

The cabana, which has been used as a home and a tourist rental, is 485 square feet plus a large covered outdoor patio. It has one bedroom, one bathroom and an open kitchen/dining room. It also has an alarm system.

The swimming pool measures 16x8 feet and has a pump and filter.

The farm has a number of fruit trees, particularly apricots, walnuts, olive trees and peaches.

BARN & TRACTOR The barn on the property is very-well constructed, 600 square feet in area, and includes an older Fiat 400 vineyard tractor, a 1,000-liter sprayer and a 16-disc plow. The barn also has an alarm system. Watering is done by canal irrigation, and the property has full irrigation rights attached to the deed for the entire acreage.

The farm has electric and is served by municipal water.
