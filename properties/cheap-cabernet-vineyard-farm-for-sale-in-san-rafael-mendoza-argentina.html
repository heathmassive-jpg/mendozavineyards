25 Acre Farm with Fine Wine Cabernet & Table Grapes

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This 25-acre farm (10 hectares) in Las Paredes has 8.5 acres of cabernet sauvignon grapes under netting plus another 3.4 acres of table grapes (eating grapes) without netting. Located in premium Vineyard Zone 1 for fine wine production, the property is situated less than two miles from where the Bianchi Winery has its spectacular multi-million-dollar winery and vineyards.

The vineyard is priced at less than what it would cost to install the vineyard alone, and then you would have five years of care and expenses until the first year of a decent harvest.

Price of US$68K includes most closing costs, so this is roughly the estimated total the buyer will have to pay.
