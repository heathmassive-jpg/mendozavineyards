Rama Caida, San Rafael, Mendoza

Gorgeous Walled Garden-Like Property with Pool Home on Nearly One Acre, Completely Furnished

Price Just Lowered!

This picturesque walled garden property is just under one acre and extremely well-kept. It is located about 15 minutes from San Rafael in a very desirable tourist area known as Rama Caida.

The remodeled home has 3 beds, 2 baths, a separate living area and an open kitchen/living/dining room. The house is 1,100 square feet with a 160-square-foot patio. They also have a tower for Internet. At the rear of the property is a garage/workshop with electric and plumbing (705 square feet) which could be converted into a nice comfortable guest house.

The gorgeous park area is spectacularly landscaped and has a wide variety of trees, flowers and shrubbery which surround a large pool with patio. They include rosemary, lavender, cedar trees, firs, magnolia, cypress, pines and red bottle brush, etc.

Some of the large trees in the park area were planted in 1926. Fruit trees include figs, cherry, pears, pomegranates, apricots and about a dozen mature olive trees that can be harvested for eating olives or traded for olive oil at one of the local olive oil factories.

There are small stores, shops and groceries in the area, with city shopping, home supply stores, banks and restaurants 15 minutes away in beautiful tree-lined San Rafael.

The owners have lived their dream here during the summer months, and often longer, since 2008. They are older now, enjoyed their Argentina adventure, and are returning full- -time to Canada for health reasons.
