Colonia Elena, San Rafael, Mendoza

Wine Company with 69 Acres of Malbec & Cabernet on 121-Acre-Farm

Farm Has 2 Wells & Irrigation Pond!

This 49-hectare (121-acre) farm in La Resolana is adjacent to a paved road about 16 miles from the center of San Rafael, in Colonia Elena, Mendoza.

There are 28 hectares (69 acres) of fine wine grapes under netting

11 hectares (27 acres) Malbec - 11 hectares (27 acres) Cabernet Sauvignon - 6 hectares (15 acres) Bonarda

The farm has canal irrigation water rights for the entire property and two irrigation wells with a large retaining pond. One well can pump 150,000 liters of water an hour and the other can deliver 280,000 liters at a depth of 60 meters using a submersible pump.

Fifteen hectares (37 acres) have structure in the form of posts and wire (see photos) for high netting that can be used for more vineyard, orchard, olive trees, pistachios or annual vegetables. Another 10 hectares (25 acres) are fallow land.

The farm also has 15,000 young vines being grown so they can be planted when needed as replacement vines.

The owners market the grapes in the following three ways

1) They turn the grapes into wine under the label "La Resolana" and sell on the domestic market to wine stores, etc.

This past year they produced 60,000 bottles for sales and distribution.

2) Some of the grapes are elaborated into wine for bulk sales.

3) The also sell some of the grapes to local wineries so they have money for immediate operating expenses.

There is worker house that could be renovated as an owner home, a barn and two tractors plus equipment (See Photos).
