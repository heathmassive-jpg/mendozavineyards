Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael, Mendoza

36 Acre Farm with Fine Wine Vineyard and Plum Orchard

Includes Farm Equipment

This farm in Cuadro Benegas just outside of San Rafael is fully planted with Malbec and Chardonnay vineyard plus an orchard of French D'Agen plums for prunes.

The property has a house for the worker and a small barn of 400 square feet. The house is an old adobe structure with two bedrooms and kitchen, dining room and bathroom.

Equipment includes a Massey Ferguson tractor, a 14-plate tractor disc, a 600 liter-sprayer, and another 200-liter sprayer.

There are 3 hectares (7.5 acres) of Chardonnay grapes in full production, 3.7 hectares (9 acres) of Malbec grapes in full production and about 4 hectares (11 acres) of newly planted Malbec which are not producing yet. There are 3 hectares (7 acres) of plums that produce an average of 30,000 kilos per year. The Chardonnay average about 20,000 kg per year and the Malbec average about 30,000 kg per year.
