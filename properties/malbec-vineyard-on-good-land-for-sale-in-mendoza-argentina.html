42 Acres of Malbec Grapes on 50-Acre Farm with House

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This 50-acre (20 hectares) farm has 42 acres (17 hectares) of fine wine Malbec grapes located in Calle Larga near San Rafael.

The grape vines are between 8 and 10 years old so all are now in full production. The owner currently sells them to the Sierra Pintada wine cooperative.

There are deeded water rights for the entire acreage via adjacent canals, and there is a deep irrigation well with an electric pump that ensures there is never a shortage of water.

The entire vineyard is watered by drip irrigation, which cuts down on maintenance costs and conserves water. The vineyard is also covered in anti-hail netting.

It would cost more than US$375K to plant the vineyard, dig a retaining pond and irrigation well, install an automated drip irrigation system and hook-up three-phase electric. This is if you got the 50 acres of land for free. Then you'd have about six years of worker salaries and maintenance costs before full harvest.

The owner says maintenance costs are now running about US$1,000 a month, or US$12,000 per year. So at current prices he needs about 30,000 kilos of harvest to break even. In a good year the farm delivers a harvest of more than 200,000 kilos.

In the 2022-23 season there was severe frost affecting almost every vineyard in Mendoza as well as other provinces. Most farms lost up to 80 percent of their normal harvest and some lost everything. 2023-2024 was a year of recuperation, so the last harvest on this vineyard was about 80,000 kilos instead of the 200,000-plus kilos expected. Nonetheless, it still produced a good positive cash flow.

There is also a house with garage/barn attached to the back for tools, plus an outdoor brick barbecue grill -- all constructed in 2011. The house by itself measures 700 square feet, is served by municipal water and is not currently occupied. It is a brick structure and could be enlarged.

An old Deutz tractor is included, which is good enough to do the job.
