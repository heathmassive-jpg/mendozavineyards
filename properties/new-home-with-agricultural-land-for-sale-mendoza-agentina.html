New Home on 28 Acres of Farm Land with Nice View, 6 Weeks from Completion

Great Balcony View!

This 28-acre farm (11 hectares) is located in Las Malvinas near San Rafael, Mendoza.

The house will take six weeks to complete, and needs to sign off on the work completed each Friday as part of the process.

Completion of the house is already paid for. High-grade ceramic tiles are already installed, but the new owners will be able to select the paint colors inside and out, which the contractor will complete to their liking.

The house is approximately 185 square meters (1935 square feet) with an additional 28.28 square meters semi-covered and a 16 square meter terraza.

The 1-1/2 story home has two bedroom downstairs with one bath and an upstairs bedroom or studio (the owner is an artist and wanted a studio with a view). There is an indoor garage with the identical ceramic flooring which is installed throughout the rest of the home. This was built to be their dream home, but due to severe medical issues they were forced to return to the USA.

Only items that need to be purchased will be a hot water heater, water pump for the storage tank and a sink (because there are different styles and makes).

Water is at the house, as is new electricity.

The kitchen cabinetry was custom- built and is in storage with high-impact aluminum-framed windows and wooden doors, along with some appliances, etc.

There are many mature trees on the property, particularly at the front where there is a forested park area. They are apricots, wild asparagus, a cherry tree, pomegranates, some olive trees, a couple peach trees, wild plums and some old grape vines.

The property has full water irrigation rights with an irrigation canal along one side, but also has an internal irrigation canal which runs through the property (see photos).

There is an old house on the property that should be torn down, or turned into something else. The old structure is not currently hooked up to water or electricity.
