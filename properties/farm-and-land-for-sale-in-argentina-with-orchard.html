24-Acre Farm in Plums & Peaches

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This 9-hectare (22 acre) farm on a paved road just outside San Rafael has 7 hectares (17 acres) of plums in good production. It also has peaches and some walnut trees.

There is a brick home on the property in good condition that can be easily renovated to serve as an owner's home. It has a kitchen-dining room, 2 bedrooms and a large storage area for a vehicle or farm equipment.

The property in on a paved road just about 5 minutes from the San Rafael airport. It has electricity and municipal water.

Irrigation rights attached to the deed also provide abundant water, and the owner's have begun excavating an area of the property for a retaining pond that would be filled from the canal water that serves the farm.

The property has a view to the south of the Sierra Pintadas (Painted Mountains) and good soil.

There are no other farms priced this low in this area.
