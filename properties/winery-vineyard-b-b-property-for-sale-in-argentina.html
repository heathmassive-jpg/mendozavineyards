Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael, Argentina

Winery with Vineyard and/or Good Income-Producing Bed & Breakfast: Starting at

Great Cash-Flow Opportunity!

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This sale is for two different adjoining properties which can be purchased separately or together.

This is a bed and breakfast tourist property with owner quarters which operates 6 months a year by owners who only reside in Argentina for six months.

This is an excellent money-maker, and the owners are able to live a very good lifestyle from the income. There are three units which fetch about $150 a night when rented and and the owners report they enjoy an occupancy rate of about 85% from October 15 to May 15. Ninety percent of the guests are return customers.

The garden area is lovely and a peaceful place where the guest frolic in the pool or do outdoor asados (barbeque). There are various fruit trees on the property and the total size is about 2-1/2 acres.

The maintenance building with tourist apartments is more than 100 years old. The current owners renovated the historic building, and decorated it with beautiful antique furniture and other special touches which you will learn about when you see this estate in person.

This adjoining property is 3 hectares (7 acres) of Malbec vineyard that totals about 8000-10,000 vines.

The winery is a 1000-square-foot facility, fully equipped, that produces 15,000 bottles of wine a year from the vineyard harvest. The owner says he does about $75,000 a year in wine sales at $5 to $6 a bottle here in Argentina.

The sale also includes an apartment (66-square-feet) that he is building above the winery which will have a spectacular view of the Sierra Pintadas (Painted Hills) to the south. (SEE LAST PHOTO BELOW FOR PROGRESS)
