Rama Caida, San Rafael, Argentina

Sweet Rural Pool Home on Pretty, Quiet & Private One Acre

This property has sold. It stays here as a record of what has moved and at what price. Ask Byron about anything comparable.

This 2 bedroom, 2 bath home is located on one acre in Rama Caida, a popular tourist area just outside the town of San Rafael in Mendoza, Argentina.

It has 1,539 square feet and features a kitchen- dining room, a laundry room and a gallery that can be used as a garage. It has a 10- by 20-foot fiberglass pool and a outdoor barbecue grill. The house was built 15 years ago.

The property is served by municipal water and electrify and has a nicely-landscaped park with a variety of plants and trees thanks to one hour a week of canal irrigation rights and a well they use for watering.

The property is about 15 minutes from the center of San Rafael and about 10 minutes from the Valle Grade Reservoir where you have fishing, boating and camping.
