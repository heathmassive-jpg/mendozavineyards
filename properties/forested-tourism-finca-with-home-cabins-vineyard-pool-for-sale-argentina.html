San Rafael, Mendoza, Argentina

Beautiful Forested Tourism Finca with Cabins, Pool & Vineyard

Located on a Paved Road!

This picturesque tourist property just over 6 acres (2.5 hectares) is located on a quiet paved road about 15 minutes from San Rafael in Cuadro Benegas, a tourist area.

Property taxes are low and currently run less than $11 a month (USD $126/year).

The main two-story house is about 2,200 square feet with one guest bedroom upstairs with a bath and private entrance and three bedrooms with bath on the ground floor.

There are three spacious rental cabins, each with a capacity of five guests and two garages plus an outdoor barbecue for visitors.

Principal rental periods are in the summer tourist season, particularly January and February, when rates are $60 a day for two people, $80 for three people and $100 for an occupancy of four or five. There are scattered dates of visitors during other months of the year, particularly July during the winter holidays and ski season where rates are $30 each per cabin. Income has been running about US$14,400 a year, according to their accounting.

In full operation, with heating in winter and air conditioning in summer, the expenses are running about $172 a month when the rentals are full and $14 a month for water, but those costs are only incurred when paying guests are present, not every month.

They also have a part-time gardener to handle ground maintenance who they pay $85 a month.

The small vineyard has 13 rows of Malbec vines and two rows of seedless table grapes. The vineyard can produce about 500 bottles of wine, and this year they had a local boutique winery elaborate the wine for them.

There are eight very large mature olive trees that produce olives for oil and one of those trees is the criollo variety that can be used for table olives.

There is an assorted variety of fruit trees on the land, including apricots, apples and figs, plus a small orchard of about 30 plum trees. They also have a few patches of raspberries here and there.

Ornamentals include spectacular large oaks, eucalyptus, cedars and arabias. Lots of roses (100 additional planted this year) plus jasmine and assorted ornamentals that cover the main garden area.

There are solar panels which provide about 20 percent of the property electricity needs and could be expanded if desired. They use the solar panels to pumps and filter for the swimming pool, with excess available for other use on the property.
