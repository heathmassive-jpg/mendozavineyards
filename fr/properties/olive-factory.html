Cuadro Bombal, San Rafael, Mendoza

Boutique Olive Oil Factory on 20-Acre Farm with Tourist Cabins & Orchards

Farm Also Produces Natural Cosmetics!

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This beautiful farm is located about 15 minutes from San Rafael, but off the main road so entrance is on a country lane rather than a paved road.

The husband and wife team currently manage the production with limited hired help.

There are three tourist cabins with barbecue grills for asados and a pool that are operated during the summer and occupied for about 90 days per year, which could be expanded with advertising. They currently rent for about US$65 a night. Two are one bedrooms (500 sq ft) with a capacity of 3 guests and the third is a 2 bedroom cabin (550 sq ft) with room for 5 guests.

The farm has an old adobe building artistically renovated into a production facility for homemade olive oil and other natural products. They have a 29-page catalog and a website they are currently updating.

Please email us for the catalog and we can provide more in-depth info on the farm and business upon request.

They olive oil equipment can press 9 to 10 liters of oil per hour and currently they produce about 2,000 liters of top- quality cold-press olive oil which they sell retail for about USD $17 per bottle. The amount of oil pressed could be expanded somewhat, but to increase the amount substantially would take investing in additional presses.

Currently all the products are sold in Argentina, but exporting would be a possibility for someone with experience or contacts to do so. The owners have not explored this option but have had the opportunity to expand into Chile and Paraguay with the cosmetics line.

Products are all natural, without chemicals, and include

Cold-pressed Extra-Virgin Olive Oil - Infused Olive Oils with Chimichurri, Rosemary, Lemon, Basil, etc. - Walnut Oil - Apricot Seed Oil - Poppy Flower Oil - Almond Oil - Rosehip Oil - Honey - Tomato Sauce - Infused Vinegars - Moisturizing Creams from Olive Oil, Apricot Seed Oil, etc. - Lip Cream - Shampoo to Mitigate Hair Loss - Skin Conditioner - Sun Screen - Natural Deodorant - Olive Oil Soap - Essential Skin Oils from Olives, Apricot Seed, Plums, etc. - Natural Toothpaste - Natural Anti-inflammatory Cream - Natural Insect Repellent - Baby Oil - Lavender Oil

The farm has an olive grove, an apricot orchard, a plum orchard, quince trees, some walnuts and a vineyard of malbec grapes with produce more than 250 bottles of wine for personal use and which they use in the homemade products they sell.
