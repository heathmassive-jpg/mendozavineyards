Cuadro Benegas, San Rafael

Boutique Winery, Vineyard and Tourism Project with Restaurant on 225 Acres

Includes a Lovely 6-Acre Lake!

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This spectacular piece of property in Cuadro Benegas outside San Rafael, Mendoza, is located across the road from the multi- million-dollar Algodon Golf and Wine Estates, which this developer also built.

He is selling the development in a picturesque estate which includes vineyards and orchard with a 6-acre lake, restaurant and a brand new boutique winery which will probably be finished by the time you read this. A hotel could also be built on the site if desired.

ALONG HIGHWAY 144 ON THE ROUTE TO THE SKI RESORT

There is 3200 feet of road frontage along Route 144, which is the road from San Rafael to Malargue and the Las Lenas ski resort.

Infrastructure includes 3-phase electic and drip irrigation for the vineyards for watering and fertilization. The lake has a dock and is 12-foor-deep.

Room and ample water for a lot more vineyard, olives or orchard if deisired.

5 acres (2 hectares) of Malbec Cot were planted in 2017.

3.7 acres (1.5 hectares) of Malbec, Cabernet Franc, Bonarda, Chardonnay and Syrah also planted in 2018.

6 acres (2.5 hectares) were installed in 2019 with Malbec, Chardonnay, Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Bonarda.

There are 6 acres (2.5 hectares) of large mature apricot trees and various fruit trees: plum, peach, apple, pear, cherry, almond, etc.)

There is a thatched-roof restaurant of 1880 square feet with stone floor, kitchen and bathrooms.

A new winery is under construction with which will be completed any day now. The winery has a total of 2,700 square feet (250 m2), which includes two cellars of 387 square feet (36 m2) each and a 775-square-foot tasting room (72 m2) with a beautiful view of the lake. The winery includes a 345-square foot mezzanine(32 m2) for the wine laboratory and offices. Two bathrooms/changing rooms are being built for workers and visitors separate from the winery.

There is a warehouse and work office of 540 square feet with bathroom.

A caretaker home has a living-dining room-kitchen with fireplace and 2 bedrooms and 1 bathroom.

The property has good soil and located on high land in an area that produces excellent wine from canals that feed the land with snow melt from the Andes and a view of the Painted Hills known as the Sierra Pintadas.
