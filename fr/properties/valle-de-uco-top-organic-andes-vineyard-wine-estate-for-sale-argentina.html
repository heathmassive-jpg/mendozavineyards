Dream Organic Fine Wine Vineyard with Striking View in Valle de Uco

Location, Location, Location!

Finca La Tranquila is an organic vineyard estate discreetly located in the heart of the Valle de Uco, just minutes from Vista Flores, in Tunuyan, Mendoza. The area is internationally recognized for the production of premium wine grapes and has become one of the most sought-after terroirs in Argentina.

Varieties include Malbec, Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir, Petit Verdot and Cabernet Sauvignon.

This year the Pinot Noir grapes were purchased by Chandon for champagne-style wine. The past few years the bulk of the grapes were selected by Domaine Bousquet, the number-one organic winery in Argentina. In previous years grapes were also sourced to Vina Cobos, the winery founded by famed international wine maker Paul Hobbs.

There is increasing tourist demand in Mendoza for luxury hotel stays at vineyards and wineries. This location in Vistaflores has the best views of the Andes and is spectacularly positioned for that level of tourism, yet there is almost nothing in the area, so demand goes unfilled. The vines and views of this estate are what tourists who wish to stay on a vineyard are hoping to find.

At sunset the Andes light up behind the vineyard creating a uniquely serene and intimate setting.

Dry Creekbed and Campo Land for Building & Recreation

The estate totals 81 acres (33 hectares) and has 40 acres (16 hectares) in fine wine grapes. Another 16 acres (6 hectares) are leveled and well suited for more vineyard, olives, nut trees or orchard.

The vineyard is planted primarily with Malbec (78.25%), complemented by Cabernet Franc (12.25%), Pinot Noir (7%), Petit Verdot (1.5%), and Cabernet Sauvignon (1%). Current production is placed with leading Mendoza wineries, reflecting consistent quality standards and professional vineyard management.

The vineyard is certified organic by LETIS, an Argentine company that approves organic vineyards for national and international standards of quality and sustainability. The vineyard would not need to remain organic, and production would increase, but organic certification gives it a competitive niche in the market and organic grapes generally command higher prices than non-organic grapes.

Watering is done by a pressurized drip irrigation system aided by a picturesque retaining pond and it has full canal irrigation rights for 54 acres (22 hectares).

In addition to its agricultural foundations, the property includes select outdoor areas designed for private gatherings and informal entertaining, as well as paths and open spaces that highlight the surrounding natural landscape. There are 16 acres of open land and forest that could be used for building projects or recreation.

The estate is well suited for a low-density, high-quality project integrating wine, gastronomy and discreet hospitality.

The property offers open views of the Andes Mountains, providing a strong sense of place and long- term landscape protection. Irrigation water is sourced from pure Andean snowmelt.

The estate enjoys exceptional privacy, naturally buffered by surrounding rural properties, ensuring a quiet and uninterrupted setting -- a characteristic reflected in its name, Finca La Tranquila.
