500-Acre Cattle Ranch with Owner's Home & Alfalfa Fields

This 500-acre cattle ranch is located about 25 minutes southeast of San Rafael near the pueblo of La Llave.

It totals 500 acres (200 hectares) and 250 acres (100 hectares) are cleared and leveled and 125 acres (50 hectres) are planted.

Deeded water rights for the entire acreage, plus an irrigation well with a 100 HP electric pump, allowing the use of a hydraulic pivot. Flow rate of 430,000 liters/hour. There are currently only 50 head of cattle on the property, but it has enough alfalfa and sorghum, etc., for 170 head. You could double that or more by putting the rest of the acreage in production. There are corrals, loading chutes, etc. I deal for an animal project, it is located on the livestock route.

There is a nice owner's home with a tiled floors, 2 bedroom and one bathroom about 1,300 square feet with a large outdoor covered patio. There is also a barn and storage areas for equipment. A separate house serves as accomodations for an employee and is currently occupied by a worker.
