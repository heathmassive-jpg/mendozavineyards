Premium Fine Wine Vineyard with Wine-Making Set-up in Valle de Uco, Mendoza

113-Acre Estate & Award-Winning Wine

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This wonderfully well-kept vineyard estate, located in the stunning Uco Valley on Route 89, in Gualtallary, sits in the heart of Mendoza, world-renowned for exceptional wines.

The property boasts a thriving vineyard cultivated under ideal conditions with prestigious grape varieties producing high- quality wines. Beyond its viticultural potential, the estate offers breathtaking views of the majestic Andes Mountains in an idyllic setting.

The current owner sells some of the grapes to help cover farming costs and has another portion of the grapes elaboated into premium high-end wine at a local Mendoza winery with a good reputation.

The vineyard has a trained team ready to remain on board. If the new buyers want to produce their own wine, the current owners would help offer elaboration services, and may possibly be interested in buying grapes from the vineyard going forward to complement their current wine sales business. Alternatively, if the new owners want to go in a different direction, the current owners can help with advice regarding wineries that can handle the elaboration and offer recommendations in that respect.

There are some proprietary wine tanks at the winery where the wine is currently produced which can also be purchased separately to aid in continuing wine production.

The vineyard features 13 grape varieties, combining iconic names like Malbec, Cabernet Sauvignon, and Chardonnay, with rarer ones like Viognier, Petit Verdot, Gewurztraminer, and Roussanne - yielding complex wines that showcase the outstanding terroir of the region. 1. Strategic Location Premier Wine Region of Mendoza

Located in Gualtallary, a prestigious sub- region of the Uco Valley, this vineyard shares its terroir with internationally renowned producers like Salentein and Rutini. Land here is limited and highly sought after. Investing now secures a position in a rapidly appreciating micro-region.

2. Fully Operational with Turnkey Expansion Potential

With 30 hectares already planted, two water reservoirs, solar energy, and existing permits for a winery and tourism facility, this estate is ready to scale. The groundwork is laid, so no need to start from scratch.

3. Diversified Revenue Potential. Beyond wine production, this estate offers

Tourism income from the planned restaurant and visitor center. - High-value wine exports, leveraging existing varietal diversity. - Olive oil production from 80 mature olive trees. - Potential for eco-retreat or agri-tourism development on the undeveloped acreage.

Equipped with solar power, drip irrigation, and a recently modernized vineyard structure (70% of vineyard posts renewed), the property ensures long-term operational efficiency and eco-compliance - essential for conscious consumers and investors.

The buyers have the option to make their own proprietary wine, and the owners will provide a licensed elaboration services with prices quoted per kilo of grapes and elaborated per per client specifications (alcohol content, blends, oaking and aging, etc.).

Once the wine is ready in bulk, the buyer can collaborate in the purchase of bottles and other materials to label, cork, bottle and pack the product, splitting the bill. This ensures better prices from suppliers, and when bottled the wine can be stored in a climate-controlled warehouse or shipped directly to buyers and distributors.

6. Attractive Price Relative to Global Wine Regions

Compared to Napa Valley or Tuscany, Mendoza offers exceptional land value and climate consistency. This vineyard provides European winemaking conditions at a fraction of the cost, with better water access and low regulatory friction.

This vineyard in Gualtallary is a rare investment opportunity in one of the most prized wine regions in Mendoza. With direct access to Route 89, it is ideally located to attract wine tourists and passionate consumers

Exceptional terroir and climate ensure the production of premium wines. - Renovated infrastructure (vineyard, warehouse, house) supports efficient operations. - Native trees, fruit trees and olive groves create a harmonious and sustainable setting. - Solar energy and water reservoirs reflect a modern eco-conscious approach. - A winery and tourism project is already approved for the site with plans and permits, providing a turnkey development opportunity.
