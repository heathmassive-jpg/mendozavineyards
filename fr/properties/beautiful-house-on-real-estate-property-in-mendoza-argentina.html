Beautiful Pool Home on Half Acre in Tourist Zone

Currently Operated as a Tourist Rental!

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This beautiful home on one-half acre (2,000 sq mts) in Pueblo Benegas near San Rafael is currently being operated as a tourism rental property and is receiving top reviews.

There are two bedrooms, one bath, with lots of light and large windows to provide spectacular views. A gorgeous pool sits just off the patio, which has a built-in barbeque grill for wonderful asados.

The furniture is included, down to plates and dishes, so you truly have a lovely home ready to move into today!

The home is located in a secure and private location. The home is about 1/2 off a major highway. Pueblo Benegas is located about a mile from the multi- million-dollar golf-wine estate Algodon, and a number of very nice restaurants are situated nearby. It is 20 minutes from the very center of San Rafael with good shopping much closer.
