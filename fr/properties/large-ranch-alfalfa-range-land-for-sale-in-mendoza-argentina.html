La Resolana, San Rafael, Mendoza

4,690-Acre Ranch for Alfalfa, Cattle & More

US$342 Per Acre!

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This ranch with land in alfalfa is located 20 miles (32 km) from the city of San Rafael in the Resolana area, with access by asphalt.

There is a total of 4,690 acres (1900 hectares) with 865 acres (350 hectares) suitable for cultivation and the rest is natural campo land. The campo land could be put into cultivation by clearing and leveling.

Since its beginnings the property has been cultivated with pasture and forage for livestock.

The 865 acres is subdivided into plots of 15 to 25 acres (6 to 10 hectares), which facilitates the distribution of livestock and rotation regarding forage.

This acreage has an appropriate water system which allows them to efficiently irrigate plots and allows livestock rotation to be compatible with irrigation rotation.

The raw campo part of the farm, is currently used for breeding and raising livestock.

The land could be put into other types of agriculture, such as vineyard and orchard or row crops, etc., but might need additional irrigation wells, and would absolutely need so if the undeveloped acres were cleared.

There is a borehole for underground water with a retaining pond (it should be cleaned and deepened at present), which would allow for the storage of 30 million liters. The property has canal irrigation rights for 100 acres, so they use channel water in addition to well water for irrigation.
