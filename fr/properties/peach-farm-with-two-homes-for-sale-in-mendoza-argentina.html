25 Acres with Peach Orchard, Open Land and Two Houses

Six Miles from Town on Paved Road

La description complète de ce bien est encore en anglais. Nous les traduisons une par une. En attendant, demandez à Byron sur WhatsApp ou par e-mail — il vous racontera tout en français.

This 10-hectare finca (25 Acres) is located with great access just 6 miles from the center of town on a paved road with all services and about 600 feet of road frontage.

Three acres have a peach orchard, most covered in netting and the orchard is in beautiful condition. The January 2025 harvest was 40,000 kilos (88,000 pounds).

The farm has about 25 olive trees which average about 2,500 kilos a year. The owner trades them for oil at the local Yancanelo olive oil factory.

The sale includes the tractor and all the necessary equipment. A neighbor farms the peach orchard in exchange for a percentage of the harvest.

There are 5 hectares (12-1/2 acres) that could be put into other crops. Previously the owner had them in row crops, vegetables.

The owner lives on the Atlantic coast of Argentina, and when in town he stays in the small guest house with his wife.

The main house is rented until June of 2025. It has 2 bedrooms, 2 baths, a large covered outdoor porch/patio. It has wood ceilings, porcelain tile floors, etc. I don't have photos because it is currently occupied.

There is a 12x8-meter barn (1,000 sq. ft.) attached to the main house.

The farm also has some grapes, peaches and nectarines for personal use. All of it beautiful.
