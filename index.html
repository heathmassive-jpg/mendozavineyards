Where the land is

Mendoza is a big province. Byron works three corners of it, and they are not interchangeable — the soil, the water and the price per acre are different in each.

  • San Rafael 37 for sale Byron’s home ground and the widest choice — farms, campo, vineyards and houses, from US$69,900 up.
  • Valle de Uco 5 for sale High-altitude fine-wine country — Gualtallary, Vista Flores, Tunuyán. Four of the five are vineyards.
  • General Alvear 2 for sale Big open campo east of San Rafael. Where acreage gets cheap — 25,000 acres here work out at US$64 an acre.

Buying here as a foreigner

You can own land in Argentina outright. Here’s how it actually goes.

Foreigners buy rural property in their own name, no local partner required. Deeds are signed before an escribano, title is searched, and water rights transfer with the land. I walk clients through every step — and I’ve done it for my own farm.
