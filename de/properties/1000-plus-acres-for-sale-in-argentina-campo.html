General Alvear, Argentina

25,000-Acre Campo on Paved Highway with 5 Windmills & Electric

Piped in Mineral Water!

Die vollständige Beschreibung dieses Objekts liegt noch auf Englisch vor. Wir übersetzen sie eine nach der anderen. Fragen Sie Byron in der Zwischenzeit über WhatsApp oder E-Mail — er erzählt Ihnen alles auf Deutsch.

This property is just about 10,000 hectares (25,000 acres) on the northeast corner of Route 143 and Route 90. It is priced at US$160 per hectare.

It has five windmills with pumps for water, plus both standard electric and three-phase electric at the road, which is an asphalt highway.

The entire property is fenced, and there are two worker houses in need of repair.

There is also access to piped in mineral water from Punta del Agua at the corner of the property.
