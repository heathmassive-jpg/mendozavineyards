Las Malvinas, San Rafael, Mendoza

Lush 90-Acre Working Farm Producing Corn, Tomatoes, Alfalfa with Cattle Corral

Lots of Water!

Die vollständige Beschreibung dieses Objekts liegt noch auf Englisch vor. Wir übersetzen sie eine nach der anderen. Fragen Sie Byron in der Zwischenzeit über WhatsApp oder E-Mail — er erzählt Ihnen alles auf Deutsch.

This is a 90-acre farm (36.5 hectares) located about 35 minutes from San Rafael in Las Malvinas, an area noted for row crops, alfalfa and which currently has the highest cattle development in the county.

There is a well-maintained employee house, a barn, a cattle chute, water troughs, and a loading ramp for livestock. It includes paddocks for dividing the livestock and crop areas, with electric fencing. The perimeter fencing is in good condition. There are corrals and chicken coops. It has a retaining pond for irrigation that currently is not in use because current water from canal irrigation is sufficient.

The farm is served by potable water and electricity services.
