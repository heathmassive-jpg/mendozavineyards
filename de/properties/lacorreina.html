San Rafael, Mendoza, Argentina

31 Acres With View of the Painted Hills, Great Water

PRICE REDUCED! GREAT VIEW!

Die vollständige Beschreibung dieses Objekts liegt noch auf Englisch vor. Wir übersetzen sie eine nach der anderen. Fragen Sie Byron in der Zwischenzeit über WhatsApp oder E-Mail — er erzählt Ihnen alles auf Deutsch.

Lots of deeded irrigation water (20 hours) on this beautiful farm in the La Correina section of San Rafael, Mendoza.

This finca is located on asphalt a 15-minute drive from the city of San Rafael and 15 minutes from the heavily touristed destination of Valle Grande -- which opens up all sorts of tourist potential in addition to agricultural production, horse-riding, etc.

There is a worker house on the property and the owners are in process of doing some upgrades.

There is electic service on the farm, and an occupied worker's house which is in poor condition with a small barn.

The great value here is the land -- 13 hectares -- the good soil, climate and leveled land perfect for all sorts of projects. Land is suitable for vineyard, orchard, olives, alfalfa walnuts, figs, annual vegetables, etc.

The current occupant of the farm thinks the best use for future purchaser would be to plant agriculture where trees are now in front right section. A new home could be built behind that and privacy trees to separate workers house. The remaining area they think would be good for cattle as they have a water source and it is is corralled.

The sellers are "crypto-friendly" and also open to receiving payment in the USA.
