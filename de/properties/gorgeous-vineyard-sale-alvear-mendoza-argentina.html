This beautiful 40-hectare farm (100 acres) with two homes has 30 hectares (75 acres) in fine wine grapes just outside General Alvear.

The grapes are 75% syrah and 25% malbec. The vineyards are watered by drip irrigation and covered in anti-hail netting.

The farm has an irrigation well and and a nice retaining pond with a capacity of 10 million liters (2.6 million gallons).

General Alvear is a city of about 30,000 people located 90 km (55 miles) southeast of San Rafael.
