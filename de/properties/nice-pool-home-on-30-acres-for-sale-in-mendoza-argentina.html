Nice Pool Home with Forested Park on 30 Acres

This 12-hectare farm (30 Acres) is located about 20 minutes from the the center of San Rafael one mile off a paved road.

The house has a pool and there is a a park area surrounding the home that includes a forest of about 200 tall eucalytus trees.

At least 25 acres are apt for planting. Years ago it was in vineyard and owned by a well-known winery family who harvested the grapes for their winery.

Lots of solitude, few neighbors, and a pleasant place to live as is, or put into crops such as annual vegetables vineyard, orchard or olive trees.

There are some large mature apricot trees and a few other fruit trees for personal use.
