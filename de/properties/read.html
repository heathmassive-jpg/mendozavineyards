Reduced $1 Million! Luxury Home on 210-Acre Estate with Orchard & Vineyard

Dieses Objekt ist bereits verkauft. Es bleibt hier als Nachweis dafür, was verkauft wurde und zu welchem Preis. Fragen Sie Byron nach etwas Vergleichbarem.

This 210-acre farm (86.7 hectares) just west of San Rafael has a beautiful home surrounded by vineyard and over 1/3 of the property in production.

Originally priced at $1.5 million, the owners are reducing the price to $500,000 for a quick sale.

It has an Internet tower, which produces spectacularly strong Internet via a long aerial antenna. The estate includes three houses for workers and two large storage buildings where the farm equipment is held.

Production Includes: -- 15 Hectares Plums (27 acres) -- 7 Hectares Grapes for Raisins (17 acres) -- 1700 Walnut Trees (equivalent to 22 acres) -- 25 mature olive trees, quince and apricots

The raisin grapes are white seedless Sultanas (known as Thompson Seedless Grapes in the USA) and command a much higher market price than other raisins. The entire crop is under hail netting -- a combination of parral and trellis-style. They are sold under contract for an excellent price, including picking. They typically produce about 15,000 kilos per hectare (100,000 kg total).

The plums are the French D'Agen variety, the preferred sugar plums for prune production in South America. The operation has 1.5 hectares (3.7 acres) devoted to drying racks because income is substantially higher if the fruit is sold ready as prunes as opposed to marketed fresh at harvest time to the various drying houses in the area. Annual plus harvest has been as high as 200,000 kg.

Quince harvest averages 4,000 kg per year, and harvest from apricots and olives is limited.

The walnut trees will be the biggest income producer on the farm in the future. They were planted about 8 years ago and reach full production in 8 to 10 years. This season should see a significant harvest.

Fruit and nut yields the past two years were significantly impacted by frost.

The house -- custom built by a leading local architect -- has 2 bedrooms ensuite, a kitchen- dining room and other nice features which includes a fully-equipped laundry room leading off the kitchen and 2 garages. Both garages can be converted to bedrooms if desired.

The home comes fully furnished with antique and up-market furnishings, all of the highest quality. This sale even includes the artwork on the walls.

There is beautiful travertine flooring in the living area and lovely solid wood flooring in the bedrooms. The total living space is more than 375 square meters (4,000 square feet) including the built-in garages. It was built in 2009.

The house is air-conditioned and the owners spent nearly US$30,000 to bring municipal water to the farm. There is 3-phase electric on the property.

The estate is very private and secure, yet just a 12-minute drive from the San Rafael Airport down very good roads.

The home has a security system in addition to caretakers on site. The owners say that in addition to watching over the estate, the laborers are excellent and experienced workers who are happy to stay on with a new owner.

The fruit production is watered by canal irrigation rights which are attached to the deed via the Rio Diamante. The property has 34 hours of definitive water rights, and for this reason is able to irrigate the entire property using flood irrigation. Water irrigation fees for 2022 totalled approximately US$1,000.

This sale includes the tractor and implements plus other various tools used on the farm.
