San Rafael, Mendoza, Argentina

Well-Kept 29-Acre Olive & Plum Farm

Sellers Accept Bitcoin!

This 12-hectare(29.5 acres) farm has a worker house with barn and equipment included.

It has 4 hectares (10 acres) of D'Agen plums (for dried plums/prunes) that harvested about 100,000 kilos (240,000 pounds) in 2024, but climate conditions led to a bad year in 2025 with just 25% of those totals. However, the current farm administrator says the trees look good so far this year, pointing to an excellent harvest if all holds.

There are 8 hectares (almost 20 acres) of mature olive trees which had produced an and average of 80,000 kilos (176,000 of olives in previous years, but also suffered in 2024 and 2024. Again, the administrator says the flowers on the trees are superb this years, also pointing to an excellent harvest this season if all holds.

Olive varieties are arbequina and frantoio, which are for olive oil. 80,000 kilos would produce about 16,000 liters of oil. To give an idea, that would be equal to about 33,800 16-ounce bottles of olive oil. It will be a few months before valid estimates can be made, but the outlook is positive so far.

The farm is in good shape, well kept with healthy trees.

The property has full deeded water rights sufficient for the agriculture presently planted on the farm.

A John Deere 2030 tractor is included, along with implements and a 500-liter pull-behind sprayer for the tractor. It is an old tractor, but has been renovated and works perfectly well to do the job.

The farm is located on a major highway and minutes from the city of San Rafael.

DRYING RACKS: This is where the fresh plums are put in wood trays on top the wire and dried in the sun.
