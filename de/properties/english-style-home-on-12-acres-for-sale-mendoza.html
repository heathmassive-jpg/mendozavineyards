Rama Caida, San Rafael, Argentina

Landscaped English-Style Pool Home on 12 Acres

Agriculture Potential in Tourist Area

This house is located in Rama Caida about 15 minutes from the center of the town of San Rafael.

It is located on a paved road with full services and includes 12 acres of good land apt for planting in vineyard, annual vegetables and orchard, or for use as horse pasture, etc.

The house has 2,150 square feet, a kitchen, laundry room, a dining room with a wood stove, a living room, 3 bedrooms, a main bathroom and a service quarter for a domestic. There is city water, irrigation rights for the farm, electric and natural gas piped in.

The lawn and garden area around home is more than three-quarters of an acre with trees over 80 years in age. The garage for tools, etc., is about 650 square feet.
