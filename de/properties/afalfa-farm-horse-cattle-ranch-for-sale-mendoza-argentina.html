Las Malvinas,San Rafael, Mendoza

Beautiful 155-Acre Farm in Alfalfa for Horses, Cattle, Crops: US$600K (Negotiable)

Owner's Home with Vineyard, Orchard

Die vollständige Beschreibung dieses Objekts liegt noch auf Englisch vor. Wir übersetzen sie eine nach der anderen. Fragen Sie Byron in der Zwischenzeit über WhatsApp oder E-Mail — er erzählt Ihnen alles auf Deutsch.

This is a 155-acre farm (63 hectares) located about 30 minutes from San Rafael in Las Malvinas, an area noted for row crops, alfalfa and cattle.

The property has a worker home and an owner home of about 900 square feet with 2 bedrooms and one bath, plus a patio and outdoor brick barbecue grill.

There is a small orchard with a variety of trees including walnuts, almonds, apricots, peaches, etc. They have a small vineyard. The soil is prized for pasture like alfalfa, potatoes, garlic, onions and those type of crops.

Almost the entire property is planted in alfalfa except for the areas around the homes, and there are two dug retaining ponds (currently dry and not used because current water needs are more than sufficient).
