Las Paredes, San Rafael, Argentina

Just Reduced $60K! 37 Acres with Vineyard, Home, Tourism & Organic Wine Sales

Local and Online Wine Sales

Die vollständige Beschreibung dieses Objekts liegt noch auf Englisch vor. Wir übersetzen sie eine nach der anderen. Fragen Sie Byron in der Zwischenzeit über WhatsApp oder E-Mail — er erzählt Ihnen alles auf Deutsch.

This 15-hectare farm in Las Paredes has 8 acres of fine wine grapes -- half Cabernet Sauvignon and half Bonarda, as well as 1 acre of new walnuts which are between 5 and 7 years old. This is a Zone 1 vineyard property with excellent soil, and the grapes are under expensive netting. The farm also has approximently 40 young olive trees.

The vineyard produces organic wines marketed under the wine label Halito. This includes a Cabernet Sauvignon and a Pet Nat Bonarda.

Pet-nats fall under the category of sparkling natural wine, wine that sees minimal to no intervention during the winemaking process so they often have that hazy color and less- filtered flavor that many find desirable.

Their Pet Nat Bonarda is described as "Super light fresh wine, very fruity and with a festive touch and fine bubbles."

The Cabernet Sauvignon is described as "A wild expression of a young wine to be enjoyed without aging in wood, aromatic on the nose, intense and balanced on the palate."

The owner home is built "Casa de Campo" style with exposed wood beams, new appliances, 3 bed- roooms, large bath, living room, kitchen and a wood stove. It has city water, electric and Internet access. There is a barn about 650 square feet and large shade trees and attractive plants landscaped around the house so it's very pleasant.

The owners are using it as a tourist venue and allow guests to stay for extra income.

There are 7.5 acres of fallow land, leveled and ready for planting. Additionally there are 15 acres of abandoned common grapes which still have the structure and could be ripped out and replanted.

The farm has excellent water rights -- and gets first irrigation from the cement canals that feed the area.
